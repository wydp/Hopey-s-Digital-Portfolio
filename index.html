01 / About

About Me

Tacurong City, Sultan Kudarat, Philippines
hopepadilla95@gmail.com
2nd Year · BS Midwifery

I chose midwifery because I believe every woman deserves safe, respectful, and empowering care during one of life's most significant journeys. Growing up, I witnessed how quality maternal health support can transform experiences and outcomes — and this inspired me to pursue a career where I can make a tangible difference.

My practice is guided by the belief that care should be holistic, inclusive, and rooted in evidence-based practice — always prioritizing the dignity, choices, and safety of women and their families. As a student at Sultan Kudarat State University, I have built a strong foundation in clinical skills, health education, and community health principles.

My goal is to become a competent community midwife who can serve rural and underserved areas, ensuring that maternal and newborn health services are accessible to all. I am dedicated to continuous learning, collaboration, and advocating for policies and practices that improve health outcomes for mothers and children everywhere.

Outside of midwifery, I enjoy traveling and exploring new cultures — though I love travel but dislike flying! I am a fan of medical K-dramas and value time spent with family and friends who keep me grounded and motivated.

02 / Philosophy

Core Values & Philosophy

The principles that guide every aspect of my practice and patient interactions.

Woman-Centred Care

Placing the woman at the centre of every decision, honoring her choices, voice, and autonomy throughout her care journey.

Evidence-Based Practice

Grounding all clinical decisions in current research and best practice guidelines to ensure the highest standard of care.

Compassion & Dignity

Approaching every patient with genuine empathy, warmth, and an unwavering respect for their dignity and emotional wellbeing.

Cultural Safety

Recognizing and respecting the unique cultural backgrounds, beliefs, and needs of every individual and community I serve.

Empowerment & Advocacy

Empowering women to take an active role in their health and advocating for their rights within the healthcare system.

Quality & Safety

Committed to delivering care that is safe, effective, and continuously improving through reflection and professional development.
